Discover the Beauty of Pakistan

Pakistan is a land of extraordinary contrasts and unmatched natural splendour. From the snow-capped peaks of the Karakoram and Himalayas in the north — home to five of the world's fourteen 8,000-metre mountains — to the golden sand dunes of the Cholistan Desert in the south, this country holds wonders at every turn.

Ancient civilisations flourished along the Indus River thousands of years ago, leaving behind archaeological treasures such as Mohenjo-daro and Harappa. Today, Pakistan blends that deep historical legacy with bustling modern metropolises, colourful bazaars, and some of the most hospitable people on earth.

PakVista was created to showcase the very best of Pakistan's tourism: the Mughal grandeur of Lahore, the turquoise lakes of Hunza, the coastal energy of Karachi, and the lush green valleys of Swat. Whether you are planning your first visit or rediscovering a homeland, we hope these pages inspire your next journey.

Lahore

The cultural heart of Pakistan. Home to the magnificent Badshahi Mosque, the Lahore Fort, and the walled city's labyrinthine food streets — Lahore pulses with Mughal history and modern energy in equal measure.

Hunza

Nestled in the Gilgit-Baltistan region at an altitude of 2,438 metres, Hunza Valley is renowned for its dramatic mountain scenery, ancient Baltit Fort, vibrant cherry blossoms in spring, and the legendary Attabad Lake.

Explore Hunza →

Karachi

Pakistan's largest city and economic hub sits on the Arabian Sea coast. Clifton Beach, the National Museum, Burns Road's legendary street food, and a thriving arts scene make Karachi endlessly fascinating to explore.

Explore Karachi →
🌿

Swat

Often called the "Switzerland of Pakistan", Swat Valley in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa offers lush pine forests, cascading rivers, trout fishing, ancient Buddhist stupas, and breathtaking green meadows at Malam Jabba.
