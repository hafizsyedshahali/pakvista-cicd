Lahore — Heart of Pakistan

Explore culture, history, food, and famous attractions.

Introduction

Lahore is one of Pakistan's oldest and most historic cities. It is known for architecture, food, gardens, markets, and cultural heritage.

Famous Places

  • Badshahi Mosque
  • Lahore Fort
  • Minar-e-Pakistan
  • Shalimar Gardens
  • Walled City

Food Culture

Lahore is known for traditional Pakistani food. Popular dishes include:

  • Biryani
  • Karahi
  • Nihari
  • Halwa Puri
  • BBQ

Culture

Lahore hosts festivals, literary events, and historical celebrations. It is widely considered a cultural center of Pakistan.

Why Visit Lahore

Visitors experience historic architecture, traditional food, shopping markets, and local culture.
