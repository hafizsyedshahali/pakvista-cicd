About Hunza

Hunza Valley is one of Pakistan's most beautiful tourist destinations, located in Gilgit-Baltistan. It is famous for mountains, lakes and historical landmarks.

Rakaposhi Viewpoint

Rakaposhi Viewpoint offers a breathtaking view of the 7,788-meter-high Rakaposhi mountain.

Attabad Lake

Attabad Lake was formed in 2010 and is known for its stunning blue water and boating activities.

Baltit Fort

Baltit Fort is a historic fort that reflects the cultural heritage of Hunza Valley.
